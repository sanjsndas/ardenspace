Cookies Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how Arden Space Concepts ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. It describes what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control their use.

Our approach in brief: We use only functional cookies necessary for the website to operate correctly. We do not use cookies for advertising, cross-site tracking, or analytics that identify individual users. We do not share cookie data with third parties.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to the site owners. Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser).

Similar technologies include web beacons, pixels, and local storage. For simplicity, we refer to all of these as "cookies" in this policy.

2. How We Use Cookies

Arden Space Concepts uses only functional cookies—those that are strictly necessary for the website to function properly. These cookies do not collect personal information for marketing or analytical purposes.

Specifically, we may use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Preference memory: To remember whether you have dismissed an informational banner or notification, so that we do not show it to you repeatedly during the same browsing session.
  • Website functionality: To support basic features such as navigation and ensuring that the site displays correctly across different devices.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

The table below provides a summary of the cookies we may use on this website:

Cookie Name Purpose Type Duration
arden_notice_dismissed Records that you have dismissed a notification or informational banner so it is not shown again. Functional Session / 30 days
arden_preferences Stores your display preferences (if any) for the website, such as font size or layout settings. Functional 30 days
arden_session Maintains your session as you navigate the website, ensuring basic functionality works correctly. Functional Session

We do not use:

  • Advertising or targeting cookies
  • Third-party analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Social media tracking cookies
  • Cross-site tracking cookies

4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not allow third parties to set cookies on our website for advertising, tracking, or profiling purposes. If we embed external content (such as a video from a third-party platform) in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, obtain your consent before any such cookies are placed.

5. Your Choices and Controls

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences through your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies from all sites
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Please note that if you choose to block all cookies, some parts of this website may not function properly. For example, we may not be able to remember your preference to dismiss a notification, and it may reappear on each visit.

For more information about how to manage cookies in your specific browser, please refer to your browser's help documentation or visit allaboutcookies.org.

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. Because we do not track individual users across websites, and we do not use tracking cookies, our website does not respond differently to DNT signals. We do not track our readers.

7. Changes to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
