About Arden Space Concepts

Independent editorial publication · Est. 2019

Arden Space Concepts is an independent publication about architecture and interior design. We write long-form, considered articles for people who care about how spaces work—not just how they look.

What we are: An independent editorial resource. We are not an architecture firm, a design studio, or a marketplace. We do not sell products or services. We publish writing.

Our Purpose

Good design is rarely about decoration. It is about light, proportion, materials, and the way a room makes you feel when you walk into it. Our purpose is to explore these ideas in plain language, without jargon and without the breathless tone that dominates so much design media.

We write for homeowners, students, and professionals who want to understand the thinking behind a space—why a window was placed where it was, why a particular material was chosen, how a floor plan shapes the way a family lives.

What We Write About

Our articles fall into six broad categories:

  • Architecture — Residential and public buildings, with a focus on how design decisions affect daily life.
  • Interior Design — The arrangement of rooms, furniture, and objects, and the principles that make interiors work.
  • Materials — Wood, stone, concrete, glass, and textiles: how they age, how they feel, and when to use them.
  • Lighting — Natural and artificial light, and its role in shaping atmosphere and function.
  • Space Planning — Floor plans, circulation, storage, and the practical geometry of living.
  • Modern Homes — Contemporary residential projects, reviewed with a critical eye.

Editorial Independence

Arden Space Concepts is independently published and reader-supported. We do not accept sponsored content, paid placements, or affiliate commissions. When we mention a product, material, or practitioner, it is because we believe it is relevant to the article—not because we were paid to include it.

Our editorial team operates separately from any commercial interests. If we ever change this model, we will disclose it clearly and prominently.

“The best rooms are not the ones that photograph well. They are the ones that feel inevitable—as if they could not have been arranged any other way.”

— From our editorial guidelines

How We Work

Every article we publish goes through a simple but rigorous process:

  • Research. We read plans, talk to practitioners, and visit buildings wherever possible.
  • Writing. We write in clear, plain English. We avoid marketing language and unnecessary technical terms.
  • Editing. Every piece is reviewed by at least one other editor for accuracy, clarity, and tone.
  • Correction. If we get something wrong, we correct it publicly and promptly.

Who We Are

Arden Space Concepts is run by a small editorial team with backgrounds in architecture, writing, and publishing. We are based in Portland, Oregon.

Our Values

We believe that writing about design should be:

  • Clear. If a sentence needs to be read twice, we rewrite it.
  • Honest. We praise what works and say so when something does not.
  • Useful. Every article should leave the reader with something they can apply or think about.
  • Independent. Our only obligation is to our readers.
