About Arden Space Concepts

An independent editorial publication devoted to the serious consideration of architecture and interior design.

We publish long-form articles that examine the principles, materials, and decisions that shape the built environment. No sponsored content. No listicles. Just considered writing for people who care about how spaces work.

Our Origins

Arden Space Concepts began as a personal archive of architectural notes—observations collected over years of visiting buildings, reading plans, and speaking with the people who make spaces. What started as a private discipline slowly became a public one.

We launched as a publication in 2024 with a simple conviction: that serious writing about buildings and interiors has value. Not product reviews. Not trend reports. Not aspirational photography with thin captions. What we wanted—and what we continue to publish—is writing that takes architecture and design seriously as disciplines, with their own histories, logics, and standards of excellence.

The name "Arden" comes from the forest in Shakespeare's As You Like It—a place of clarity, reflection, and the occasional unexpected encounter. "Space Concepts" is deliberately plain. We are interested in how spaces are conceived, made, and inhabited. The concepts matter more than the branding.

Editorial Philosophy

We believe that architecture and interior design are disciplines—not merely aesthetic preferences. A building is not a picture. A room is not a mood board. Both are physical environments that shape how people live, work, rest, and relate to one another. Writing about them requires attention to proportion, material behavior, spatial logic, and the accumulated wisdom of practitioners.

Our articles are written to be useful for years rather than weeks. We do not chase trends, and we do not publish content because it might perform well in search engines. We publish articles because we believe the subject deserves careful treatment and because we have something substantive to say about it.

Every article is researched, fact-checked, and edited by our team. Where we make claims about materials, methods, or historical precedents, we cite our sources or draw on direct professional experience. Where we offer opinions—and we do—we identify them as such.

Editorial Team

Our team includes architects, interior designers, and experienced design journalists. We commission articles from practitioners who can write with authority about their areas of expertise, and we work closely with contributors to ensure clarity, accuracy, and depth.

What We Stand For

Our editorial values are straightforward, and we hold ourselves to them in every article we publish.

  • 01

    Independence

    We accept no sponsored content, native advertising, or paid placements. Our editorial decisions are made without commercial influence. We have no financial relationships with manufacturers, developers, or design firms.

  • 02

    Depth

    We publish long-form articles because complex subjects require space. We would rather publish one carefully considered piece than five superficial ones. Our articles are written to be read, not skimmed.

  • 03

    Accuracy

    We fact-check our articles and cite our sources. Where we are uncertain, we say so. Where we are offering opinion, we identify it. Our readers deserve to know the difference between reporting and commentary.

  • 04

    Usefulness

    We write for people who design, build, renovate, or simply care about spaces. Our articles aim to be practical as well as thoughtful—useful to professionals and accessible to engaged general readers.

  • 05

    Humility

    Architecture and design are vast fields, and no publication can cover everything. We are honest about what we know and what we do not. We are always learning, and we welcome correction.

What We Don't Do

Just as important as what we publish is what we choose not to publish.

We do not publish sponsored articles, advertorials, or "partner content." We do not accept payment for coverage. We do not run display advertising. We do not use affiliate links.

We do not publish listicles, "top ten" roundups, or clickbait. We do not chase viral trends or optimize our headlines for social media algorithms. We do not publish articles simply because a topic is popular.

We do not claim to be the final word on any subject. We are one publication among many, and we are grateful for the work of other writers, critics, and publications who take architecture and design seriously.
