About Arden Space Concepts

Arden Space Concepts began as a personal archive of architectural notes and grew into an independent publication with a simple conviction: that serious writing about buildings and interiors has value. We are not a design firm, an agency, or a marketplace. We are a publication.

Our editorial approach is grounded in the belief that architecture and interior design are disciplines—not merely aesthetic preferences. We write about proportion, material behavior, spatial logic, and the accumulated wisdom of practitioners. We are interested in why spaces work, not just how they look.

The publication is edited by Margaret Ellsworth, a former editor at Architectural Review and Dwell, with contributions from architects, designers, and writers across Europe and North America. Our articles are researched, fact-checked, and written to be useful for years rather than weeks.

We have no commercial relationships with manufacturers, developers, or design firms. Our only obligation is to our readers.
